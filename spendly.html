Spendly

Track every rupee. Plan every payoff.

A lightweight personal finance app built on Flask and SQLite. Log expenses and see where the money goes, plan your way out of debt with snowball or avalanche strategies, and get an educational long-term outlook on any US stock from a multi-agent Claude pipeline.

Request access

Expenses & profile

Add, edit and delete expenses by category and date. The profile page totals your spending, shows the top category and a per-category breakdown, filtered by any date range.

Analytics

Stat tiles for total spent, average per expense and per month, a monthly trend, and your five largest expenses — for all time or any window.

Debt payoff planner

Track each debt's balance, APR and minimum payment. Compare snowball, avalanche and custom orders with an extra monthly payment, and see your debt-free date, total interest and interest saved. Log payments to watch each balance fall.

Trend Analyst

Enter a US ticker and a multi-agent Claude pipeline reads prices, SEC filings and news, then writes a plain-English long-term outlook with confidence, key indicators and 1- and 2-year ranges. Educational only — it never tells you to trade.

Built with

Python Flask SQLite Jinja2 Vanilla JS Anthropic Claude pytest

No ORM and no front-end framework — raw parameterized SQL, server-rendered pages and hand-drawn SVG charts. Built with Claude Code.
