FAQ

Questions, answered

LumiqBrain is an open-source initiative building AI systems at the intersection of large language models and telecom infrastructure — including LLMOS and ASK3GPP.

LLMOS is an AI-native operating system layer that lets you interact with OS-level operations, automation, and intelligence using natural language, powered by an extensible agent framework.

ASK3GPP is a RAG-powered Q&A engine for 3GPP telecom standards — ask a question in plain English and get precise, spec-grounded answers instead of manually hunting through documentation.
